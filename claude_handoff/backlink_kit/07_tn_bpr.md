# Tennessee BPR (Board of Professional Responsibility)

- **Platform:** Tennessee Board of Professional Responsibility (the state regulator that maintains the official attorney registry)
- **URL:** https://www.tbpr.org/
- **Attorney lookup:** https://www.tbpr.org/attorney-search
- **Account type:** Mandatory. Every TN-admitted attorney has a BPR profile. The lawyer doesn't "claim" it the way directory listings work — they update their record via the BPR's online portal as required by Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 9.
- **Audience:** Bar regulators, opposing counsel doing conflict checks, anyone who searches the public BPR lookup
- **SEO value:** Modest direct-backlink value, but the BPR is an authoritative source. Search engines treat the data as canonical for the lawyer's basic info.

## Step-by-step

1. Log into the BPR portal at https://www.tbpr.org/ → Member Login (uses BPR number + password).
2. Navigate to **"Update Contact Information"** / **"Annual Registration"**.
3. Update the fields below. **All updates are required to be filed with BPR within 30 days of a change** (Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 9, § 10.2).
4. The BPR record is what other directories pull from. Updating BPR first → propagating to other directories second is the correct order.

## What this is NOT

- The BPR does NOT publish a marketing-style "bio." It publishes name, address, phone, email, bar number, admit date, status (Active / Inactive / Suspended / Disbarred), and any disciplinary history.
- There is no "About me" field. No headshot. No practice areas to pick. Just the canonical contact facts.

## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs

- None. The BPR's fields are factual (NAP + bar status). No marketing copy is collected.
