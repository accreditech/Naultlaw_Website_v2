# Sumner County / Gallatin Area Chamber of Commerce

- **Platform:** Sumner County Chamber of Commerce (the chamber serving Gallatin, Hendersonville, Portland, White House, Westmoreland, and surrounding Sumner County communities)
- **URL:** https://www.sumnerchamber.org/
- **Membership URL:** https://www.sumnerchamber.org/join (likely; verify current URL)
- **Account type:** Paid membership. For a solo attorney, the entry-level tier is typically $300–500/year. Provides a directory listing, networking-event access, and ribbon cuttings / chamber social signals.
- **Audience:** Local Sumner County business owners (potential clients), local press, local government / civic connections
- **Decision:** **Verify business value before paying dues.** Chambers are most valuable when the lawyer attends events and uses the listing as a relationship-funneling tool, not a passive backlink.

## Step-by-step

1. Visit https://www.sumnerchamber.org/ — confirm the current join / membership URL.
2. Email or call the chamber to request the current member-tier pricing. Solo professional / 1–5 employee firms get the lowest tier.
3. Submit the membership application + payment.
4. After processing (1–2 weeks), the chamber emails directory-listing credentials.
5. Log in → complete the directory listing fields below.
6. Optional: schedule a ribbon-cutting (free for new members), which generates a press release + photo + chamber social-media post.

## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs

- Chamber directory categories typically use **"Attorneys"** / **"Legal Services"** — compliant.
- Chamber directory bios are short (200–500 chars). The trimmed bio below is the right length.
- Chamber events sometimes invite members to "speaker" slots. If Steve accepts, the speaker bio used to introduce him also needs RPC 7.1 review.

## Cost-benefit note

Sumner County Chamber membership has solid local-SEO and
relationship value for a Gallatin-based solo firm targeting local
business clients. The ROI depends on attendance — passive membership
without showing up at events is a weaker investment.

**Recommendation:** Verify current pricing first. If under $500/year
and Steve is willing to attend at least one monthly event, claim.
Otherwise, skip and revisit when bandwidth allows.

## Alternative or supplemental chambers to consider

- **Gallatin Area Chamber of Commerce** — Gallatin-specific, may overlap with Sumner County Chamber. Confirm which (or both) is the right fit.
- **Hendersonville Chamber of Commerce** — separate Sumner-County-municipal chamber for Hendersonville. Lower priority unless Steve is targeting Hendersonville-specific clients.
- **Tennessee Chamber of Commerce & Industry** (statewide) — more expensive, less local-SEO value for a solo firm. Skip.
