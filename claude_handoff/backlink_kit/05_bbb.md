# Better Business Bureau (BBB)

- **Platform:** Better Business Bureau — Middle Tennessee region
- **URL:** https://www.bbb.org/us/tn/middle-tennessee
- **Claim URL:** https://www.bbb.org/get-listed (free unaccredited listing) OR https://www.bbb.org/accredited-business-application (paid accredited listing)
- **Account type:** Two tiers:
  - **Unaccredited (free):** Listed in BBB's directory; no accreditation badge; can still receive complaints / reviews
  - **Accredited (paid, ~$300–600/year for solo firms):** Get the badge; access to BBB tools; appears with the BBB Accredited seal
- **Audience:** Consumers; some clients still treat BBB accreditation as a trust signal
- **Decision:** **Verify business value before paying dues.** Many modern solo firms skip BBB; some still see it as worthwhile. Steve decides.

## Step-by-step (unaccredited, free)

1. Visit https://www.bbb.org/get-listed
2. Search for the firm name. If a listing already exists (BBB sometimes creates one from public records), click "Claim Listing."
3. Provide verification — typically firm email + phone match.
4. Fill in the fields below.
5. Save.

## Step-by-step (accredited, paid)

1. Visit https://www.bbb.org/accredited-business-application
2. Application asks for ownership info, years in business, references, and compliance attestations.
3. BBB conducts a business-credit and complaint-history review (~2 weeks).
4. If approved, an invoice for the annual fee arrives. Pay → badge becomes available.
5. The badge is a downloadable image + an embed code Steve can put on the firm site (footer is the standard placement).

## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs

- BBB's "Business categories" field uses **"Attorney"** / **"Lawyer"** / **"Legal services"** — none of those trigger RPC 7.1.
- BBB's Customer Reviews are testimonial-flavored. If Steve sets the listing to accept reviews, those reviews are NOT bar-disclosable testimonials under RPC 7.1 Comment [3] as long as they appear on BBB's surface, not the lawyer's own marketing. Steve should NOT re-publish BBB reviews on naultlaw.com without source attribution; if attribution + context is preserved, it's the same accepted form as Google/Yelp testimonials on the homepage.

## Headshot / Photo specs

BBB displays a business logo, not a headshot. If Steve has a firm logo, upload that. If not, leave blank or use a wordmark version of "Nault Law."

## Cost-benefit note

If Steve is on the fence about BBB accreditation: the free unaccredited
listing is a low-cost backlink and worth claiming. The paid accredited
badge is a judgment call — for solo law firms targeting business and
real-estate clients (mostly referral-driven), the badge's marketing
lift is modest. Recommendation: claim the free listing first, then
decide on accreditation after a quarter of seeing whether BBB-sourced
inquiries materialize.
