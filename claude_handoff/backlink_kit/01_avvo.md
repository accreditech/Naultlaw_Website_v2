## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs on Avvo specifically

- Avvo's free profile has an **"Areas of practice"** picker. The list is fine to use — Avvo's labels are descriptive (e.g., "Business," "Real estate"), not "specializations."
- Avvo will display "Practice areas," not "specialty." Compliant.
- Do NOT use Avvo's optional **"Avvo Rating"** or **"Top Attorney"** language in the bio itself. The badge is generated by Avvo; the lawyer doesn't claim it. Bio copy that says "Top-rated attorney" trips the comparative-claim restriction even if Avvo's algorithm assigned a rating.
- Avvo's free-text "About me" field is the bio. Use the Ready-to-paste copy below.
