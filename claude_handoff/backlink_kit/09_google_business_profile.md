# Google Business Profile (GBP)

- **Platform:** Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business)
- **URL:** https://www.google.com/business/
- **Manage URL:** https://business.google.com/
- **Account type:** Free
- **Audience:** Anyone using Google Search or Google Maps — by far the highest-volume "local pack" surface for any consumer-leaning lookup like "Tennessee attorney near me" or "Gallatin business lawyer"

**This is the single highest-leverage profile on this list.** Local-pack rankings drive disproportionate consumer inquiries. Prioritize this above every other directory.

## Step-by-step

1. Visit https://business.google.com/ and sign in with the Google account that controls the firm's GA4 + Search Console (likely the same account Steve uses for naultlaw.com administration).
2. Search for "The Law Office of Stephen Nault" or the office address. If a listing already exists (Google sometimes auto-creates them from third-party data), click "Claim this business."
3. Verify ownership — options are:
   - **Postcard** (most common): Google mails a verification code to the office address. 5–14 business days.
   - **Phone**: Google calls the office number. Steve enters the code displayed on the call.
   - **Email**: Google emails the verification code (only available for some categories).
   - **Video** (newer): Steve records a short video showing the office signage, the address, and proof of being inside. Reviewed by Google within ~5 days.
4. Once verified, complete every field below. **Filling 100% of the profile fields is a known ranking factor.**

## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs

- GBP's primary category picker uses **"Attorney"** / **"Lawyer"** — compliant.
- GBP's secondary categories let you add sub-areas; pick from the list (e.g., "Business attorney," "Real estate attorney"). These are GBP's labels, not "specialty" claims. Compliant.
- GBP's "Business description" field (750 chars) is bio-style copy. Use the trimmed bio below. **Do NOT write "specialist" or "specializes" in this field** — Google has been known to flag attorney profiles that use these terms, and the resulting suspension is annoying to undo.
- GBP allows customer Q&A and reviews. Reviews are NOT in Steve's control to write, but he should claim the profile and respond to reviews professionally. RPC 7.1 governs the lawyer's response language too — no "Thanks, I'm glad we won your case!" responses; instead "Thank you for the feedback" is the safe form.

## Photo specs

GBP wants multiple photos:

- **Logo:** 720×720 px PNG, transparent background, max 5 MB
- **Cover photo:** 1080×608 px JPG (16:9), max 5 MB. Use the office building exterior or a clean professional shot.
- **Profile photo:** 720×720 px JPG square (Steve's headshot)
- **Additional photos:** Office interior, conference room, building exterior with signage. 5–10 photos is the recommended floor.

## Why this matters more than the others

GBP listings drive Google Maps results, Local Pack results (the 3-pack
that appears at the top of many local searches), and influence
organic-search local relevance signals. For a Gallatin-based solo
attorney targeting local business and real-estate clients, this is
the single highest-leverage SEO asset on the list.

Estimated time to claim + complete: 30 minutes initial; 5–14 days for
postcard verification; ongoing maintenance is ~15 minutes/month
(respond to reviews, post occasional updates).
