## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs on Justia specifically

- Justia's free profile categorizes by "Practice areas," not "specialties." Compliant.
- Justia offers a free "BadgeMaker" widget to embed on the firm site. Optional. If used, only embed it AFTER the profile is live to avoid linking to a 404.
- Justia Q&A: Justia syndicates Q&A answers and the lawyer's profile gets the byline. Good authority signal but RPC 7.1 applies to those answers too — Steve should not write "I'm a specialist in …" in a Q&A response.
