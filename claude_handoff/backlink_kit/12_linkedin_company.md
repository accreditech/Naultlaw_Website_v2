# LinkedIn — Company Page

- **Platform:** LinkedIn Company Page (separate from the personal profile in `11_linkedin_personal.md`)
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/
- **Account type:** Free (LinkedIn Pages tier; no fee, but creator must be admin of a personal profile)
- **Audience:** Business clients researching the firm; referring attorneys; press; recruiters (if Steve ever hires)
- **SEO value:** Company pages rank for the firm name. A complete company page ranks above a partial one.

**Decision point:** Does a Company Page already exist for The Law Office of Stephen Nault, or does Steve need to create one? `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION FROM STEVE]`. If no, the page can be set up by following the create flow below. If yes, claim and update.

## Step-by-step (create new)

1. Log into LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/.
2. From the home feed, click **Work** (top right) → **Create a Company Page**.
3. Pick the type: **Small business** (under 200 employees).
4. Fill in fields below.
5. Click **Create page**.
6. After creation, add Steve's personal profile as an admin (Manage admins → Add).
7. Add a "Verify your page" badge if LinkedIn offers it (LinkedIn Verified Company is a free trust badge).

## Step-by-step (claim existing)

1. If an unclaimed page exists, search LinkedIn for "The Law Office of Stephen Nault."
2. Click the page → look for a **"Claim this page"** option in the top right.
3. LinkedIn verifies via the personal profile's bar association with the firm (or via email verification at the firm's domain).

## TN RPC 7.1 watch-outs

- The Company Page tagline and About field collect bio-style copy. Same RPC 7.1 rules apply.
- LinkedIn Company Pages can list "Specialties" as a tag. **Skip this field or use the workaround:** LinkedIn's "Specialties" tag is the platform's wording, not the firm's claim. Some attorneys still avoid filling it because of how the rendered profile reads. Recommended: leave blank, OR fill with practice-area phrasing ("Business law, Real estate law") and note in the About field that these are practice focuses, not specializations.
