## Suggested content-gap framework

For each thin page, the writer should aim to add:

- **Tennessee-specific procedural detail** — code cites, filing venue,
  statute-of-limitations specifics, county-by-county nuance where relevant.
- **Typical engagement sequence** — what happens on day 1, what happens
  in week 1, what the client receives at each step.
- **Pricing and turnaround specifics** — flat-fee vs hourly, typical
  retainer size, typical turnaround range. Pages already have brief
  statements; the writer can expand with realistic examples.
- **Concrete fact patterns** — short, anonymized scenarios that illustrate
  when this engagement is the right call. 1-2 sentences each, 2-3 patterns.
- **"What this is NOT" framing** — keep the existing
  not-a-closing-attorney language. For other pages, similar scope-of-work
  fences where relevant.

What the writer should NOT add:

- Outcome guarantees (TN RPC 7.1).
- "Specialist" or "specialized" claims (TN RPC 7.4).
- Generic AI-generated filler that does not add information.
